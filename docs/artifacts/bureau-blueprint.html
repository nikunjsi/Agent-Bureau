Rules that never bend

15 product invariants · 6 working rules
INVARIANT 01

The conversation is the product. Someone who only uses chat must be able to finish a project.

INVARIANT 02

Nothing is built before the brief is approved.

INVARIANT 03

Write it down before you do it. Every change is recorded before the thing it describes happens.

INVARIANT 04

Employees never commit. Only the Core saves work — and the claim was rewritten downward when the strongest lock proved impossible.

INVARIANT 05

Nothing outside the workspace is readable or writable. At any trust level. Not overridable.

INVARIANT 06

Fail closed. Unclear, broken or unreachable → the safe answer.

INVARIANT 07 · 08

A timeout never causes something irreversible, and every option must state its consequence.

INVARIANT 09 · 10

Never ask what memory already answers. Every visual state maps to a real one.

INVARIANT 11 · 12

The window holds no authority, and money is whole millionths of a dollar, never decimals.

INVARIANT 13 · 14 · 15

No claim without a test. No non-commercial asset, ever. Employees never claim to be human.

WORKING RULE 1

A test may not re-implement what it verifies. Four did, and all four passed while proving nothing.

WORKING RULE 2

A guard is not a guard until something on the real path calls it. A lock on a door nobody walks through.

WORKING RULE 3

An ordering check needs a presence check first. "A before B" passes when A never happened.

WORKING RULE 4

Read known issues before diagnosing. The same environment fault was re-diagnosed from scratch five times.

WORKING RULE 5

Anything that removes the user's only way back must refuse. Never fire, park or starve the Director.

WORKING RULE 6

The same decision must not be made in two places. Both halves passed their tests; only one won.
